Project Title
Beyond the Degree: How Students and Early-Career Professionals Keep Learning

Broad Project Theme
Growth mindset, career development and personal productivity

Course Topics Linked

Week 9: Cultivating a Growth Mindset (personal productivity, personal growth and transformation)
Week 12: Personal and Professional Development (career planning, stress management, time management)

WEF Future of Jobs 2025 Core Skills Linked

Curiosity and lifelong learning
Resilience, flexibility and agility
Technological literacy

Project Description
This project explores how students and early-career professionals acquire new skills outside formal education, respond to feedback, and adapt when academic or workplace demands change. It investigates which learning habits work, what prevents consistent learning, and how individuals balance learning with their other responsibilities. Through qualitative interviews with independent learners (coding, analytics and similar skills), recent graduates, interns and junior employees, and mentors who regularly learn new tools, we will collect real experiences such as learning a tool for a project, preparing for an interview, or overcoming a difficult subject. The topic ties directly to the Growth Mindset and Personal and Professional Development weeks of SPG, and to the WEF’s emphasis on curiosity, lifelong learning, resilience and technological literacy as skills that will define employability by 2030. The project will conclude with practical recommendations for building sustainable learning habits.
